# Roadmap: AI for every "can't tell", then beyond the rules

Agreed with the user on 2026-11-26: first finish and polish the AI's answers
to **every "can't tell" the engine's rules produce**; only when that is
complete, go to the next level, **success criteria that no rule can
automate**, where the AI would raise items of its own. The decisions this
needs are listed at the end and deliberately left open.

## Where things stand

The engine has **132 checks that can end in "can't tell"** (read from the
engine itself: [ai-cant-tell-inventory.md](ai-cant-tell-inventory.md),
generated by `node docs/design/ai-cant-tell-inventory.js`; a test fails when
the engine gains a check the inventory has not placed). Each is in one lane,
by what would settle it:

| Lane | What settles it | Checks | Phase 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `answered` | The AI answers it today | 29 | Polish (below) |
| `read` | The AI, from the element and the text around it, as today | 17 | Step 1 |
| `tree` | The browser's own accessibility tree, no model | 23 | Step 2 |
| `walk` | The AI, from the focus or open walk, or a small extension | 12 | Step 3 |
| `measure` | A measurement by the lab, then the AI or a person | 8 | Step 4 |
| `picture` | The AI, once it can see the picture | 1 | Step 5 |
| `media` | Audio, video or time | 5 | Step 5 |
| `interact` | Using the page further (errors, announcements) | 3 | Step 5 |
| `code` | The markup states it; a person confirms | 32 | Decision 3 |

The lanes are a judgement made from each check's documented reasons; move a
check when its reasons say otherwise.

## Phase 1: every "can't tell" from the rules

### What "done" means for a check

1. The evidence that settles it is collected (read, walked, measured).
2. Its question is in `registry.js`, or its measurement settles it outright.
3. Its facts show in **Review AI answers** (`keyFactRows`), so a person can
   settle what the AI leaves open.
4. A case with its correct answer in the demo (`expected.json`).
5. Evaluated blind on real pages with documented answers; 30 to 50 cases
   before the check's agreement is trusted (`ai:agreement` marks fewer).
6. In the user guide's tables, and its lane changed to `answered`.

### The order

**Polish what is answered** (in START-HERE's Next): a person's labels on the
evaluation runs; a run through the lab's own Claude call with a real key
(prompt caching); more cases for link text (6), field labels (3) and bold as
heading (8); the open walk's open points (a real dialog that cannot be
closed, hover menus, arrow keys).

**Step 1: `read` (34 checks).** Questions like today's, by family, each
family one change with its demo cases:

- *Text alternatives:* `svg-text-alternative-quality` (the most frequent in
  the runs: 97; **done 2026-11-26**, `registry-8`), `area-alt-quality`, `input-image-alt-quality`,
  `input-image-alt-decorative` (**done 2026-11-26**, `registry-9`), `object-`, `embed-`,
  `canvas-text-alternative-quality`, `image-redundant-alt`,
  `video-poster-text-alternative-present` (**done 2026-11-26**, `registry-10`;
  best-practice findings are asked since, see the eval README).
- *Names and labels* (**done 2026-11-26**, `registry-11`, with what the
  checker reported): `heading-quality`, `form-control-label-quality`
  (2.4.6), `label-in-name` (2.5.3), `control-name-title-only`,
  `link-name-quality-link-only` (2.4.9), `identical-links-same-purpose`,
  `identical-iframes-same-purpose`, `landmark-unique`.
- *Structure:* `form-group-name-present`, `form-control-single-label`,
  `table-fake-caption`, `td-has-header`, `table-th-has-data-cells`,
  `empty-table-header`, `heading-order`, `bypass-blocks-present`,
  `page-zones-landmark`, `region`, `focus-order-semantics`,
  `presentation-role-conflict`, `accesskeys`.
- *Is it text, does it matter:* `contrast-minimum`, `contrast-enhanced`,
  `symbol-graphic-contrast` (the ratio is measured; the question is whether
  the symbol is text or carries information), `css-orientation-lock`.

**Step 2: `tree` (23 checks).** "Name present" checks that end in "can't
tell" when the name may come from a closed shadow root, a custom element's
internals or content nested too deep. Chrome's accessibility tree (the
DevTools protocol) has the computed name: no model needed, and a name found
can then go to the matching `read` question. Decision 4 is where this lives.

**Step 3: `walk` (12 checks).** Evidence the walks already collect or nearly
do: `tabindex` (what Tab reaches first), `focus-not-removed-by-script`
(`lostFocus`), `iframe-focusable-content`, `scrollable-region-focusable`,
`mouse-only-event-handlers`, `css-hidden-focus`, `aria-expanded-state-mismatch`
and `aria-checked-state-mismatch` (the open walk sees what shows),
`skip-link` (press it, see where focus goes), `aria-hidden-focus`,
`nested-interactive-controls-absent`, `server-side-image-map-absent`.

**Step 4: `measure` (8 checks).** The lab measures, the AI or a person
judges what is left: `contrast-computable` (text over an image or gradient:
the pixels behind it), `link-in-text-block` (hover and focus cues, as the walk
measures focus), `text-spacing-content-loss`, `avoid-inline-spacing`,
`reflow-horizontal-scroll`, `resize-text-content-loss`, `target-size-minimum`,
`target-size-enhanced`.

**Step 5: `picture`, `media`, `interact` (9 checks).** `image-of-text` needs
pictures sent to the model (Decision 5); `video-caption`,
`media-alternative-transcript-evidence`, `no-autoplay-audio`,
`moving-content-pausable`, `deprecated-elements-not-used` need the media or
time (a pause control can be looked for by reading); `live-region-announcements`,
`invalid-field-error-message`, `password-paste-enabled` need the page used
further (Decision 6).

## Phase 2: success criteria no rule can automate

Not started. The AI would look for problems no check raises, so it raises
its own items (Decisions 1 and 2). Candidates, by the evidence they need:

1. **What is already read:** 3.1.2 language of parts (a phrase in another
   language without `lang`); 1.3.3 sensory characteristics ("the green button
   on the right"); 1.3.1 structure shown only visually (lists, tables or
   headings made of plain boxes); 3.3.2 missing format instructions; 2.4.10
   section headings (AAA); 3.1.3 to 3.1.5 unusual words, abbreviations,
   reading level (AAA).
2. **Pages compared within a run:** 3.2.3 consistent navigation, 3.2.4
   consistent identification, 3.2.6 consistent help, 2.4.5 multiple ways.
3. **New measurements, like the walks:** 1.4.13 content on hover or focus
   (dismissable, hoverable, persistent); 3.2.1 and 3.2.2 change of context on
   focus or input (the walks already see changes); 1.4.10 reflow, 1.4.4 resize
   text, 1.4.12 text spacing beyond what the rules check; 2.2.2 moving
   content; 3.3.1 and 3.3.3 error identification and suggestion (submitting
   forms).
4. **Seeing the page:** 1.4.1 use of colour, 1.4.5 images of text, the
   visual side of reflow (Decision 5).

## Decisions left for later

1. **AI-raised items (phase 2):** how they show in the report (for example
   "raised by the AI, not confirmed"), apart from the engine's results, and
   never counted towards conformance until a person confirms them.
2. **Whether the AI may pass a phase 2 item** or only flag it, as keyboard
   today (fail only).
3. **The `code` lane (32 checks):** the markup states the problem and the
   engine leans fail; does the AI add anything (whether a script fixes it
   later, whether it matters), or does the person confirm without it?
4. **Where the `tree` lane lives:** in the lab's AI pass, or in the engine
   (the engine computing the name from the browser's accessibility tree
   would settle these for every user of it).
5. **Pictures to the model:** for `image-of-text`, 1.4.1 and the visual
   checks; what leaves the lab grows (customer sites,
   `ai-cant-tell-data-and-agents.md`), and the cost; to be measured first, on
   the one question the model keeps getting wrong (the W3C news photo).
6. **Using forms further:** submitting with errors only on test systems,
   opt-in, as `open` is.
7. **The reading the labels follow:** WCAG's or a stricter audit's (the
   GOV.UK "Read more" links, the lightbox close button), to be settled with a
   person's labels.
8. **When a check is trusted:** how many cases, which agreement, and whether
   the confidence threshold (0.6) is per check.

## Keeping it current

- `node docs/design/ai-cant-tell-inventory.js` after the engine is updated or
  a question is added; `ai-cant-tell-inventory.test.js` fails until a new
  check has a lane, until the `answered` lane matches the registry, and until
  the generated page is rewritten.
- A step done here moves its checks' lane to `answered` and is recorded in
  START-HERE and the evaluation README.
