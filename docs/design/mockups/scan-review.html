Reviewing a run's scans

What a person does after a workflow has run, step by step: see how much of the run repeats a template, check each group by its pictures, decide what to merge, take out or exclude, see the report as it will read, and download it. Fake data: a shop of 46 scans. Everything below can be clicked; nothing is saved.

Every pattern is a groupNo choosing step: the tree lists every pattern found. Acting on one (naming, merging, excluding) is what makes it the person's.
Pictures firstEach group is a grid of its pages' screenshots, those that differ from the template first: what to take out shows at a glance.
See before doingMerging shows what it changes (scans, failures) before it is applied; every action can be undone at once.
The rest, safelyScans in no group can be shown alone, grouped by hand or excluded after a confirmation that lists them. They are never merged: unrelated pages are not a template.
